# SaleOps: Automobile Parts Sales Deep-Dive with Python
## Project Overview

<b>SaleOps</b> is the Python analytics phase of a three-part business intelligence project series built using **SQL, Power BI, and Python**.</br>

While the SQL project focused on structured querying and the Power BI dashboard emphasized KPI monitoring, this notebook uses Python for a deeper analytical exploration of automobile parts sales performance.
The objective is to uncover operational trends, revenue drivers, product performance patterns, and customer behavior through statistical analysis and advanced visual storytelling.
---
## 🎯 Project Objectives
* Analyze sales performance over time
* Identify top-performing product lines
* Compare revenue vs quantity sold
* Evaluate warehouse contribution to total sales
* Understand client type and payment behavior
* Generate actionable business recommendations

## Key Insights
### Sales Trends
* Daily sales fluctuate significantly, but overall demand remains stable.
* Moving average analysis helps reveal a consistent sales baseline.
### Product Performance
* Suspension and braking products lead in sales volume.
* Engine products show lower volume but higher average transaction value.
* Revenue is driven by both high-volume and premium-value strategies.
### Warehouse Operations
* Central warehouse is the strongest revenue contributor.
* Sales concentration suggests location-based demand advantages.
### Customer Behavior
* Wholesale clients generate the majority of revenue.
* Bank transfers are the dominant payment method.
---
## Business Recommendations
* Maintain safety stock for high-demand product lines.
* Increase focus on premium-margin products like engines.
* Investigate underperforming warehouses for efficiency gains.
* Strengthen relationships with wholesale customers.
* Optimize payment experience around preferred methods.
